# Art credits

Both images are 700-year-old public domain manuscript illuminations from the
**Codex Manesse** (Große Heidelberger Liederhandschrift, Heidelberg
University Library, Cod. Pal. germ. 848, ca. 1305–1340), sourced from
Wikimedia Commons and resized/re-encoded to WebP for this app. No
attribution is legally required (public domain / life+100 years expired),
credited here anyway as a courtesy.

- **codex-manesse-altstetten.webp** — Konrad von Altstetten, fol. 249v.
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Altstetten.jpg
  (Public Domain Mark 1.0)
- **codex-manesse-otto-brandenburg-chess.webp** — Otto IV von Brandenburg
  playing chess, fol. 13r. Source:
  https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datei:Meister_der_Manessischen_Liederhandschrift_004.jpg
  (public domain)

Used to match Medeltidsveckan 2026's actual festival theme, "en
kärlekshistoria" ("a love story") — confirmed from medeltidsveckan.se's own
homepage copy, not assumed.
